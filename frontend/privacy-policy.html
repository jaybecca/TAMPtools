TAMPTOOLS • PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 August 2026

Important: This policy is a product-ready template and should be reviewed by qualified counsel for the TAMPtools operating entity, launch countries and actual vendors before publication. Where a local law gives you stronger rights than this policy states, that law prevails.

1. Who we are

TAMPtools is a software-as-a-service platform providing digital productivity, document, AI content, email, reporting and automation tools. The legal entity, registered address and privacy contact must be completed with the operator's final corporate details before launch. General support is available at tools@tampconsulting.space.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data: name, first name, email address, authentication records, plan, trial/subscription status and account preferences.
  • Locale data: language, currency and country/region. TAMPtools may use IP-derived country information and browser language to localise the experience and route checkout. IP-derived location is an estimate and is not treated as precise geolocation.
  • Usage data: tools used, quotas, credits, generated reports, job status, timestamps and security/audit events.
  • Content: text, prompts, files, images, documents and other material you intentionally submit to a TAMPtools tool.
  • Payment information: checkout identifiers, transaction/subscription references, currency, amount, provider and payment status. Payment card/bank credentials are intended to be entered into the selected payment provider's checkout and are not intentionally stored as raw card data by TAMPtools.
  • Integration data: OAuth account identifiers and encrypted access/refresh credentials where you connect supported services.
  • Support data: messages, attachments, voice notes or other information you choose to send to support, including through WhatsApp.
  • Technical/security data: IP address, browser/device information, request IDs, logs, fraud/abuse signals and error information.

3. Why we process information

We process information to create and secure accounts; provide tools; process uploaded content; generate AI outputs; enforce plan limits; schedule and send provide reports; process subscriptions and promotions; route customers to an appropriate payment provider; prevent fraud and abuse; provide support; maintain backups; troubleshoot; measure reliability; and comply with legal obligations.

4. Legal bases

Depending on the jurisdiction, processing may be based on contract/steps requested before contract, consent, legitimate interests, legal obligations, or another lawful basis recognised by applicable law. Where consent is required, it will be requested in an appropriate manner and can be withdrawn subject to legal exceptions.

5. AI processing

TAMPtools provides an integrated AI experience. Prompts and requested content may be processed by the AI infrastructure configured by TAMPtools to provide the requested feature. TAMPtools should not be represented as guaranteeing that AI output is accurate, original, complete, non-infringing or suitable for a regulated decision. Production vendor names and data-processing terms should be listed in the final subprocessor register.

6. Email and third-party integrations

When you connect a supported third-party service, TAMPtools processes the minimum account credentials/tokens and content needed to perform the integration. You control whether an integration is connected and should review the connected provider's privacy policy. Tokens are intended to be encrypted at rest and should be revoked when an integration is disconnected.

7. Payments

TAMPtools' production payment architecture uses Paddle and Flutterwave only. TAMPtools automatically routes checkout based on country, currency, language and provider capability; customers are not required to choose a gateway. The payment provider processes payment information under its own terms and privacy notice. 8. Promotions

TAMPtools may run seasonal or promotional discounts. Promo codes may be subject to eligibility, plan, country, currency, dates, redemption limits and recurring-cycle restrictions. We keep records needed to prevent duplicate or abusive redemptions and to reconcile promotions with payment-provider transactions.

9. Sharing and subprocessors

We may disclose information to hosting/database providers, object storage, AI infrastructure providers, email delivery providers, payment providers, connected integrations, security/monitoring vendors, professional advisers and authorities where required. We do not sell personal information as a business model. A production subprocessor list should be maintained and updated when vendors are added.

10. International transfers

TAMPtools may operate across borders. Where applicable law restricts international transfers, TAMPtools will use an appropriate legal mechanism or safeguard, such as an adequacy decision, contractual safeguards or another lawful mechanism. Customers should not assume that all data remains in their country.

11. Retention

We retain data only for as long as necessary for the stated purpose, legal obligations, security, dispute resolution, accounting, fraud prevention or legitimate business needs. Uploaded temporary files are intended to be subject to a configured lifecycle policy; the exact production retention period will be stated in the Data Controls documentation and may vary by tool. Backups may persist for a defined backup-retention period before secure deletion.

12. Security

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including authentication controls, rate limiting, security headers, encrypted integration tokens, signed payment webhook verification, audit logging, least-privilege administration, backups and job retry controls. No system can guarantee absolute security.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, obtain a copy of certain data, withdraw consent, opt out of certain communications, or lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. GDPR/UK GDPR, Nigeria's NDPA, Brazil's LGPD, California privacy laws and other local laws can provide different rights and deadlines. Requests can be sent to tools@tampconsulting.space. We may need to verify identity before fulfilling a request.

14. Automated decisions and profiling

TAMPtools may use automated signals for localisation, payment routing, abuse prevention and product limits. Checkout routing is intended to select a compatible payment provider, not to make decisions about a person's legal rights or eligibility for essential services. Where applicable law gives you rights concerning solely automated decisions, those rights will be respected.

15. Cookies and similar technologies

TAMPtools may use essential cookies/local storage for sessions, theme, language and security. Optional analytics/marketing technologies should only be enabled where appropriately disclosed and, where required, after consent. The production cookie inventory should be kept in the Data Controls documentation.

16. Children's privacy

TAMPtools is not intended to knowingly collect children's personal data in violation of applicable age requirements. If a jurisdiction imposes a different age threshold or parental-consent requirement, the applicable rule will be followed.

17. Account deletion

You may request account deletion. Subject to legal, security and financial-retention requirements, account data and associated content will be deleted or anonymised according to the production retention schedule. Backups may remain temporarily until their normal expiry.

18. Changes

We may update this policy when the service, vendors or law changes. Material changes will be communicated where required. The effective date identifies the current version.

19. Contact

Privacy/support: tools@tampconsulting.space. WhatsApp support may also be offered for customer support and may receive the information you choose to send through WhatsApp.
