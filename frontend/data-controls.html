TAMPTOOLS • DATA CONTROLS

Data Controls & Retention

Version: 4.5 • Effective: 30 August 2026

This document explains the operational controls TAMPtools intends to use for personal data, uploaded content, payment events, integrations and account deletion. It complements the Privacy Policy and is not a substitute for a signed customer Data Processing Agreement where one is required.

2. Retention schedule

  • Temporary uploads: default target is 7 days unless a tool requires a different documented lifecycle.
  • Sessions: expire according to the configured session lifetime and are invalidated when appropriate.
  • Payment/accounting records: retained for the period required by tax, accounting, fraud and payment obligations.
  • Audit/security events: retained only as long as needed for security, abuse prevention, dispute handling and legal obligations.
  • Backups: retained according to the configured backup policy; deletion from the live database does not necessarily remove data from a backup immediately.
  • Deleted accounts: live personal data is deleted or anonymised after the applicable grace period, except data required for legal, fraud, payment, dispute or accounting reasons.

3. User controls

Customers can request access, correction, deletion or other applicable privacy rights through tools@tampconsulting.space. Identity verification may be required. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

4. Data export and portability

Where technically available, TAMPtools will provide reasonable export mechanisms for account and customer-generated data. Export availability may vary by tool and third-party integration.

5. Security controls

  • HTTPS should be enforced in production.
  • Passwords are never stored in plaintext.
  • OAuth access/refresh credentials are encrypted at rest.
  • Payment webhook signatures are verified before state changes.
  • Payment events are idempotent to reduce duplicate processing.
  • Admin access is role-restricted and audited.
  • Rate limiting and security headers are enabled.
  • Background jobs retry controlled failures and record terminal failures.
  • Backups should be encrypted and periodically restored in a test environment.

6. Internationalisation and IP routing

TAMPtools may process IP address and browser-language information to estimate country, select language/currency defaults and route checkout. IP geolocation is approximate and should not be treated as precise location. Users should be able to change language/currency where the product permits, and a saved preference should take precedence where appropriate.

7. Payment routing

TAMPtools routes between Paddle and Flutterwave based on provider capability and localisation signals. Payment credentials are intended to be entered into the provider checkout. TAMPtools stores transaction/subscription references needed to provide entitlements and reconcile payments, not raw card numbers.

8. AI and content controls

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal data into prompts or uploads. TAMPtools may transmit requested content to the AI infrastructure configured for the requested feature. Production vendor/subprocessor records should identify applicable processors and their contractual safeguards.

9. Email and integration controls

Customers are responsible for lawful recipient lists and content. TAMPtools should maintain suppression/unsubscribe records, provider event handling and OAuth scopes should be limited to what each integration requires.

10. Data subject requests

Requests should be routed to tools@tampconsulting.space. The operational workflow should verify identity, locate relevant records, assess legal exceptions, execute the request, record the action and communicate completion or the lawful reason for refusal.

11. Incident response

Security incidents should be triaged, contained, investigated, documented and remediated. Where a law requires notification to a regulator or affected individuals within a specified period, TAMPtools will follow the applicable legal requirement.

12. Subprocessors

A production subprocessor register should be maintained for hosting, database, object storage, AI, email, payment, monitoring and connected services. Customers should be able to identify material processors relevant to their use of the Service where required by law or contract.

13. Compliance framework

TAMPtools is designed to support privacy-by-design principles including purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, security and accountability. Applicable requirements may include the EU/EEA GDPR, UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and NDPC rules, Brazil LGPD, California privacy laws, South Africa POPIA, Canada's PIPEDA or provincial laws, and sector-specific/electronic-marketing rules such as ePrivacy/PECR, CAN-SPAM and CASL. The exact obligations depend on the operator, customer, data subject, activity and jurisdiction.

This document is not legal advice. Before launch, have counsel map the actual TAMPtools data flows and customer countries to the laws that apply.
