Dedicated proposal-first workspace with live client-ready proposal preview, client/business/project details, scope, deliverables, milestones, investment, deposit, terms, four materially distinct visual styles, currency/accent controls, local browser autosave, new/reset controls, zoom, and print-to-PDF export matching the live document content.
