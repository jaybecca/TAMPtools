# TAMPtools Phase 49 — QR Code Generator

Dedicated QR Code Generator rebuilt around static/dynamic QR workflows.

## QR types
URL, text, email, phone, SMS, WhatsApp, Wi-Fi, vCard, event, app, social, business, profile, coupon, feedback, video, images, PDF, menu, MP3/audio, multiple links, Bitcoin, PayPal.

## Tiers
- Free: core static QR types, PNG/SVG, monthly QR allowance.
- Lite: more static types, design customization and branding scaffolding.
- Pro: dynamic QR campaigns, optional logo/photo branding, scan tracking, advanced types.
- Business: Pro capabilities plus higher/fair-use volume, bulk/API/team-ready scaffolding.

## Output
PNG and SVG. Dynamic QR codes use a TAMPtools campaign URL and record scan count/last scan time.
